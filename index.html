About Me

My journey into software engineering began with a foundation in Mechanical Engineering, where I developed a systematic approach to solving complex problems through design, analysis, and critical thinking. Working with tools like SolidWorks and AutoCAD taught me the value of precision, optimization, and building systems that are both reliable and efficient.

Today, I'm applying that same engineering mindset to software development as a Full-Stack Developer and AI/ML Engineer in training at Turing Tech LLC. I enjoy building modern web applications with Golang, Python, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS , focusing on scalable backend services, intuitive user experiences, and clean, maintainable code.

I'm also expanding my expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Prompt Engineering, exploring how intelligent systems can automate workflows, enhance productivity, and solve real-world challenges. I'm committed to continuous learning and building impactful software that combines thoughtful engineering with practical innovation.
